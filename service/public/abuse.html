Report abuse

Anyone can publish a document here anonymously. If one of them is being used to harm someone, we want to know.

What to report

  • Phishing, or any page impersonating a real person, company, or service
  • Malware, or script that attacks the person viewing it
  • Content that targets or endangers a private individual
  • Anything unlawful where the page is hosted

How

Email hello@ikangai.com with ABUSE in the subject, and include the full URL. The 8-character code in the hostname — abcd1234.rewritable.ikangai.com — is what identifies the document.

There is also a machine route, if you are reporting programmatically:

POST /report/<short>  with  {"reason": "...", "contact": "..."}

What happens next

A person reads it. Reports are queued for review and never trigger automatic removal. That is deliberate: an endpoint where an anonymous reporter can unpublish someone else's document would itself be an abuse vector. Removal is an operator action.

Published snapshots expire on their own — 24 hours for an ordinary publish, 90 days of inactivity for a connected share — so many problems age out without anyone acting.

If it is your own document

You do not need us. A connected share carries an update token held only by the browser that created it, and that browser can unshare it directly from the ↗ panel.
