How Distributed Teams Ship Faster

Posted by Jordan Lee on March 3, 2026

Distributed teams that ship reliably share one habit: they write things down before they build them, so a decision made in one time zone survives being read in another.

The second habit is smaller batches. Teams that merge daily catch integration problems while the context is still fresh, instead of discovering them a week later during a painful release.

Why async reviews work

Async code review removes the tax of matching time zones. A reviewer in Berlin can leave notes a developer in Austin reads first thing in the morning, and neither person loses a night's sleep waiting on the other.

The tradeoff is latency: a review that would take five minutes synchronously can take a day asynchronously if nobody sets an expectation for turnaround time.
